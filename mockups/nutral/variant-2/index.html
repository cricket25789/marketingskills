The engagement

Four steps, roughly twelve weeks.

— Week 1 to 3

Diagnose

We pull eighteen months of invoice data and map the real shape of your contingent workforce: who you pay, what they cost, where the margins sit, and which arrangements quietly default to the supplier's favour.

— Week 3 to 6

Design

Working with your procurement, HR, finance and operational leads, we shape a target operating model — rate cards, agreed on-costs, compliance posture, and a governance layer that fits how your organisation actually makes decisions.

— Week 6 to 10

Negotiate

We open the commercial conversations agencies and intermediaries rarely volunteer. Margin transparency, rebate structures, payroll fees, billing accuracy. The objective isn't the lowest price — it's a fair one, evidenced.

— From week 10

Sustain

We hand over reporting, renewal calendars and a benchmark library that your team can run. We stay on in a light-touch advisory capacity for as long as it's useful — and only that long.
