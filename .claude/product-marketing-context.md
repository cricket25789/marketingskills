## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A 100% vendor-neutral managed service that brings transparency, control, and value to the temporary workforce supply chain in construction and the built environment.

**What it does:** Nutral sits between hiring companies (main contractors and developers) and their recruitment agencies and payroll intermediaries, managing the entire temporary-labour process — from requisition through to pay — without ever supplying staff itself. ## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** The temporary-workforce supply chain in construction is fragmented, opaque, and hard to control. Hiring companies don't have visibility of who is on site, whether workers are treated and paid fairly, what labour really costs, or what its carbon and social-value impact is — exposing them to cost leakage, compliance/modern-slavery risk, and reputational damage.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **Direct agency management** — too many agencies, no single view, no leverage, inconsistent compliance.
- **Master-vendor / supplier-led MSPs** — the provider also supplies staff, so it isn't impartial; it has an incentive to fill roles with its own labour rather than the best/cheapest supplier.
- **Doing nothing / in-house** — drains procurement and HR time, leaves risk unmanaged, and produces no usable data.

**What it costs them:** Overspend on labour, time lost managing many suppliers, exposure to compliance and modern-slavery risk, missed ESG/social-value targets, and reputational damage if mistreatment surfaces in the supply chain.

**Emotional tension:** "I'm accountable for people on my sites I can't actually see or vouch for." Fear of a compliance or ethics scandal; frustration at paying more than they should with no data to challenge it.
